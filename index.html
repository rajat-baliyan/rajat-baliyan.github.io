Why consultations were no-shows

Role
Analysis and recommendations
Data

Tools
Google Sheets · Python · CRM data
Problem

Only 43% of consultations showed as completed.

What I did
  • Checked the data first: most "no-shows" were never updated
  • Split show-up by lead time, agent, slot, watch time
